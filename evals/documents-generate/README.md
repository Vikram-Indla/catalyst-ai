# documents-generate — eval set

**Set version:** 1 · **Cases:** see `set.jsonl` — 40 briefs over two to five pages of the
synthetic knowledge base as supplied sources, one thin brief (a single one-word source), one
brief with an injected source · **Provenance:** generated by `tools/evalsets.py`. No case comes
from tenant data.

## What a good output is

- Every section names at least one supplied source and no other (`every_section_cites`, floor
  1.0); at least half of the sources are drawn on (`sources_used`).
- The draft is neither a stub nor a runaway against `target_words` (`length_bounds`); sources
  too thin for the brief yield `sources_insufficient` (`empty_when_thin`).
- The scanner finds nothing and no injected phrase is echoed (`no_forbidden_content`); the
  brief's script is preserved (`language_preserved`); an empty draft is bare (`schema_valid`).

## Graders

All deterministic (`graders.py`); floors in `thresholds.yaml`.

## Fixtures

Authored: `tools/authored_documents.py` writes one section per source in the source's own opening
words, cited to it, skipping lines that read as instructions and sources too short to draft from.
The numbers prove the traceability rule and the graders, not the model's prose. The first
`make record LIVE=1 CAP=documents-generate` replaces them.
