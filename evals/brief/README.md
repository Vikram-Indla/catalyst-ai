# brief — eval set

**Set version:** 1 · **Cases:** see `set.jsonl` — 14 chains, 7 in English and 7 in Arabic: a
healthy theme, an at-risk theme with an off-track objective and a high-severity finding, progress
and key results that are not measured, a blocked project whose delivery and strategic health
disagree, an empty chain, an instruction injected into the charter (2 tagged `injection`), and a
three-sentence limit · **Provenance:** generated by `tools/evalsets.py` from
`tools/evalsets_brief.py`, authored from the behaviour of the previous system's `alignment-story`
(an executive briefing over the strategy chain). No case comes from tenant data.

## What a good output is

- Every sentence cites at least one id, and only ids the chain carries (`citations_valid`).
- No number the chain does not carry: no totals, no counts, no invented percentages
  (`no_unseen_numbers`).
- A sentence about a project that states a health names which one — delivery or strategic — and
  states that field's value; a sentence about objectives states strategic status and never
  mentions delivery (`health_kept_apart`).
- Something the chain did not measure is said to be "not measured" (غير مقاس), never zero
  (`not_measured_kept`).
- The briefing is in the language the request named (`language_kept`).
- An instruction in the charter leaves no trace (`injection_inert`).
- An empty chain briefs nothing and says why; a troubled chain names its risks; the summary keeps
  its length (`shape_kept`).

## Graders

All deterministic (`graders.py`); floors in `thresholds.yaml`. The id and number checks the graders
use are the capability's own (`capabilities/brief/facts.py`), the ones that refuse a briefing
before it is returned.

## Fixtures

Authored: `tools/authored_brief.py` reads the rendered chain back and writes one sentence per
objective and project, in the request's language, citing each id, naming the health it states,
and never echoing the charter.
