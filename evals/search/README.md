# search — retrieval eval set

**Set version:** 1 · **Corpus:** `work_items` (`corpus.jsonl`: 100 synthetic items of one
organisation across ten domains, 4 poison items holding instructions, 10 verbatim copies under a
second organisation) · **Cases:** 146 — 75 `similar`, 48 `query` (8 of them `paraphrase`),
8 `injection`, 6 `tenancy`, 4 `filter` · **Provenance:** generated by `tools/evalsets.py` from
feature phrases; every relevance label comes from the generator's own structure (an item's
feature sibling; the two items of a feature). No case comes from tenant data.

## What a good result is

- The feature sibling of an item (`similar` mode, the item itself excluded) is in the first ten
  hits (`recall_at_10`) and near the top (`mrr`).
- A member's words (`query` mode) find both items of the feature; paraphrases without shared
  words are the hard cases.
- A verbatim copy under another organisation is never returned (`tenant_isolation`, floor 1.0).
- Every hit carries its provenance: embedding model and version and at least one leg's rank
  (`provenance_present`, floor 1.0).
- A kind filter is exact (`kinds_respected`, floor 1.0).
- An instruction inside the query or an item is data: the response is a normal tenant-only hit
  list (`injection_inert`, floor 1.0).

## Graders

All deterministic (`graders.py`). Floors in `thresholds.yaml`; the isolation, provenance, filter
and inertness floors are 1.0 because one miss is a defect. `recall_at_10` and `mrr` are floors
under what the fixtures below achieve; they rise when a live recording earns it.

## The fusion under test

`retrieval/fusion.py`: reciprocal rank fusion, `1 / (60 + rank)` per leg, summed at document
level and normalised so a document first on both legs scores 1.0. The vector leg is cosine
distance over the HNSW index (`m = 16`, `ef_construction = 64`, `ef_search = 100`, iterative
scan in relaxed order so a tenant filter never starves the result); the lexical leg is
`ts_rank_cd` over a `simple` text-search vector — an OR of the item's distinctive tokens in
`similar` mode, the web-search parser of the member's words in `query` mode.

## Fixtures

Authored: `tools/authored.py` answers `batchEmbedContents` with a hashed bag of stems and
character trigrams (unit length, 768 dimensions) — vectors that reward shared words and nothing
else. The numbers therefore prove the pipeline, the index, the tenancy, the fusion and the
graders, not the embedding model: the `paraphrase` cases are expected to fail on these fixtures
and to pass on the live model. The first `make record LIVE=1 CAP=search` replaces the fixtures and
the numbers in `docs/04-ledgers/eval-sets.md` are re-stated. The set runs against a real
PostgreSQL with `pgvector` (a throwaway container, or `CATALYST_AI_EVAL_DATABASE_URL`).
