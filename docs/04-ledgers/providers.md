# Providers and models register

Generated by `make ledgers` from `providers/*/models.py` and configuration; written by hand
until then. An alias is what a capability names; the register resolves it. A model id outside
this table fails `tools/checks/models`; a row without a retention setting fails `catalyst-ai check`
(`ARCH-002 §4`). Prices are the published list prices on the date of the row, in micro-dollars per
1 000 tokens (input / output); they are re-verified at every live recording.
